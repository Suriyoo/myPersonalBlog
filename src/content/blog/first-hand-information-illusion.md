<div class="section-heading">
<h2>看到一手信息，不代表真正理解</h2>
<p class="section-translation">Seeing First-Hand Information Doesn't Mean Understanding It</p>
</div>

社交网络让我们能够轻松接触大量一手信息，但这并不意味着我们有能力准确理解它们。

<p class="translation">Social networks make it easy to access large amounts of first-hand information. However, access does not necessarily mean understanding.</p>

一手信息通常缺少必要的背景和上下文。即使所有内容都能看懂，也可能无法理解它真正想表达的意思。

<p class="translation">First-hand information often lacks the background and context needed to interpret it correctly. Even when we can understand every word, we may still miss its actual meaning.</p>

例如，同一段视频，普通观众和行业专家可能会得出完全不同的结论。区别不在于他们看到了什么，而在于他们是否具备理解这些信息所需的专业知识。

<p class="translation">For example, an industry expert and an ordinary viewer may interpret the same video very differently. The difference is not what they see, but whether they have the expertise needed to understand it.</p>

<div class="section-heading">
<h2>脱离上下文，信息很容易被误读</h2>
<p class="section-translation">Information Can Easily Be Misinterpreted Without Context</p>
</div>

学术研究中经常出现看似矛盾的结论。

<p class="translation">Academic research often produces findings that appear to contradict each other.</p>

例如，一项研究认为某种物质可能致癌，另一项研究却没有发现相关证据。但单独看其中任何一项研究，都不足以判断这种物质是否真的致癌。

<p class="translation">For example, one study may suggest that a substance causes cancer, while another finds no evidence of such a link. However, neither study alone is enough to determine whether the substance is actually carcinogenic.</p>

我们需要把新研究放到过去所有相关研究的背景中，了解已有结论，再判断这项研究带来了什么新发现。

<p class="translation">To understand the issue, we need to consider the full body of existing research, examine previous findings, and then evaluate what the new study actually adds.</p>

只有结合完整的研究背景，才能更准确地理解一项研究的真正意义。

<p class="translation">Only by looking at research in its broader context can we properly understand its significance.</p>

很多一手信息也是如此。脱离历史和专业背景，普通读者可能只能理解字面意思，却无法理解其真正含义。

<p class="translation">The same applies to many other forms of first-hand information. Without historical and professional context, ordinary readers may understand the literal meaning while missing what the information actually implies.</p>

<div class="section-heading">
<h2>为什么会产生一手信息错觉？</h2>
<p class="section-translation">Why Does the Illusion of Understanding Arise?</p>
</div>

一手信息往往比经过转述的内容更容易让人产生信任感，而且它的字面意思通常并不难理解。

<p class="translation">First-hand information often feels more trustworthy than second-hand accounts. Its literal meaning is also usually easy to understand.</p>

这就容易产生一种错觉：我们认为自己已经直接看到了原始信息，因此也就理解了事情的全貌。

<p class="translation">This creates a misleading sense of confidence: because we have seen the original source ourselves, we assume that we understand the whole picture.</p>

但事实上，理解一手信息往往需要专业知识和大量背景信息。对于缺乏相关经验的普通读者来说，直接接触原始资料并不意味着能够正确解读它。

<p class="translation">In reality, interpreting first-hand information often requires specialized knowledge and substantial background context. For ordinary readers without relevant expertise, direct access to original sources does not guarantee accurate understanding.</p>

**获取信息和理解信息，是两种完全不同的能力。**

<p class="translation"><strong>Accessing information and understanding information are two very different abilities.</strong></p>

<div class="section-heading">
<h2>媒介的价值：把专业信息转化为公众能够理解的内容</h2>
<p class="section-translation">The Value of Media: Making Specialized Information Accessible</p>
</div>

这正是专业媒体和内容创作者发挥作用的地方。

<p class="translation">This is where professional media and content creators play an important role.</p>

例如，超导领域研究者在社交网络上发布的推文或视频，普通读者可能很难准确理解。专业媒体需要对这些内容进行解释，补充必要的专业背景，甚至采访当事人或第三方专家。

<p class="translation">For example, posts and videos published by superconductivity researchers on social media may be difficult for ordinary readers to interpret. Professional journalists need to explain the content, provide the necessary technical background, and sometimes interview the researchers or independent experts.</p>

这个过程实际上是在把专业领域的语言和背景，转化为普通公众能够理解的信息。

<p class="translation">This process translates specialized knowledge and context into information that the general public can understand.</p>

媒体的价值不只是传递一手信息，更重要的是对信息进行筛选、解释和加工，让它真正适合读者理解。

<p class="translation">The value of media is not simply in passing along first-hand information. More importantly, it lies in selecting, explaining, and processing information so that it becomes meaningful to readers.</p>

<div class="section-heading">
<h2>高质量的加工信息可能比原始信息更有价值</h2>
<p class="section-translation">Processed Information Can Be More Valuable Than Raw Information</p>
</div>

做好这类信息加工并不容易。它需要作者和编辑具备专业知识，也需要投入大量时间和精力。

<p class="translation">Producing this kind of content is not easy. It requires expertise from both writers and editors, as well as considerable time and effort.</p>

许多主流英文科学媒体采用付费订阅模式，说明确实有不少读者愿意为专业的信息解读付费。他们知道，自己很难独立处理来自 X（Twitter）、arXiv、YouTube 或 Bilibili 的大量原始信息。

<p class="translation">Many leading English-language science publications operate on subscription models. This suggests that readers are willing to pay for professional analysis and interpretation. They recognize that independently processing the vast amount of raw information on platforms such as X (Twitter), arXiv, YouTube, and Bilibili is difficult.</p>

对于普通读者来说，如果缺乏理解上下文的能力，过度关注门槛较高的一手资料，可能得到的只是大量噪音，而不是真正有用的信息。

<p class="translation">For ordinary readers, following specialized first-hand sources without the ability to understand their context may produce more noise than useful information.</p>

尤其是在社交网络时代，一手信息已经不再稀缺。

<p class="translation">Especially in the age of social media, first-hand information is no longer scarce.</p>

并不是所有原始信息都值得普通读者花费时间研究。

<p class="translation">Not every original source deserves the average reader's time and attention.</p>

相比之下，经过专业筛选、整理和解释的信息，可能更有实际价值。

<p class="translation">By contrast, information that has been carefully selected, organized, and explained by experts may offer much greater practical value.</p>
